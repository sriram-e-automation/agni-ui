Grid, rails, panes & bars

AgniUI is a desktop-first, data-dense ERP. One responsive structure governs every desk: horizontal bars frame the top and bottom, a vertical rail holds navigation, the content region runs on a column grid, and an optional right workspace rail carries cross-app shortcuts. The grid and chrome step down at three breakpoints.

Rail
Left navigation. Collapses 248 → 72 → overlay. Right workspace rail (56) for cross-app shortcuts on desktop.
Panes
Content surfaces (cards/tables). Each owns its padding (--pane-pad); the shell owns the frame.
Grid
12 / 8 / 4 columns with 24 / 20 / 16 gutters. Align panes to columns, never to pixels.

Tokens live in spacing.css (--bp-tablet, --bp-desktop, --bp-wide, --grid-cols…) and layout.css (containers, safe areas, .agni-grid + .span-*). The matching @media rules re-point rail, pane & grid tokens so any component reading them adapts with no per-component code. Gutters and margins come off the 8-point spacing scale (24 / 20 / 16 — majors + half-steps), so grid rhythm and component rhythm never disagree.

Containers, margins & safe areas

--container-sm · 640
--container-md · 960
--container-lg · 1200
--container-xl · 1440 = --content-max

sm narrow forms · md single-record detail · lg dashboards without workspace rail · xl full desk cap. .agni-container centres content at the cap with --grid-margin side margins (24 / 20 / 16 per breakpoint).

Safe areas (phone). Margins never sit inside a notch, curved edge or home bar: --page-margin-* = max(--grid-margin, env(safe-area-inset-*)); sticky footers pad by --safe-bottom. Zero on desktop — safe to use everywhere.

Adoption guide

Do
Build every desk from AppShell — header bar, rail sidebar, content, footer — so structure stays identical across modules.
Lay panes on the column grid and size with spacing tokens (--space-*, --pane-gap), not arbitrary pixels.
Let tokens drive the breakpoints — read --rail-w, --pane-pad, --grid-cols rather than hard-coding widths.
Collapse the rail to icons on tablet and to an overlay drawer on phone; keep one primary action reachable.
Reserve bars for global chrome and let panes own their padding — the shell owns the frame.
Don't
Don't invent a bespoke shell per module or nest a second app frame inside the content region.
Don't hard-code rail or pane widths in px — it breaks the responsive token swap and drifts between desks.
Don't place page-level actions or filters inside the top bar; those belong in the page title / controls row.
Don't keep the workspace rail or multi-column layouts on tablet/phone — collapse to a single column.
Don't let content page-scroll the whole shell — bars and rail stay fixed; panes scroll within.
