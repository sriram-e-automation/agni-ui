The naming rule, the merge test and the Aug 2026 merge table live in their own card — Component naming rule. In short: name the shape and not the business object, nouns never verbs, no invented role words, one name per job with capability in props.

Where a component lives

Families are named after the shape of what they hold, not the domain they serve — data/ is anything that displays records, core/ anything shape-only. The one exception is workflow/, the approval layer that genuinely knows what a request, approval and audit entry are. If a component would work unchanged in a product that has no approvals, it does not belong there.

Shape-based families, one domain familyAug 2026: erp/ was retired. Its 9 domain-bound pieces moved to workflow/; the 9 shape-generic ones (RecordCard, KanbanBoard/Card, TaskCard, ApprovalCard, PersonCard, OrgTree, BulkActionToolbar, DocumentPreview) to data/; RoleGate to core/. Folder moves don't change the runtime namespace — every component is still reached flat off the bundle.
A component is not done until its card shows its statesShipping in the bundle is half of it. Without a specimen showing variants and states, the next person re-invents the component in their page — which is exactly how the scaffold accumulated eleven local parts.
Decisions on record

Where two components could do one job, the call is written down here so it isn't re-litigated per module.

QuestionDecision
Read-only fields — FormSection variant or its own component?Its own: DetailSection + DetailList. A form section owns labels, validation and focus order; a detail section owns copyable values and an edit hand-off. One component doing both would carry two contracts.
Dated label/value cards — AuditTrail variant?No. AuditTrail is chronological and actor-driven; education/employment history is a set of label/value pairs. Those use DetailList layout="row".
Tracker cards, support options, settings choicesOne OptionRow with variant="card" | "row". ActionTile stays separate: it is a landing-page entry point, not a row in a list.
One figure — StatCard or QuickStats?QuickStats when clicking filters a records region below it. StatCard everywhere else. The strip is a filter that happens to summarise; the card is a figure.
Event colours in agenda viewsEventRow takes the shared tone vocabulary. No module maps event types to raw colours.
Nav overlay drawerNavRail overlay owns its scrim and slide-in panel. Hosts never wrap the rail in their own drawer.
Stub screensEmptyState. There is no placeholder-page component.
Attendance grid (year × month day cells)Decided Aug 2026 — stays scaffold-local. It is neither a Heatmap (which is a chart with a value scale) nor a Calendar (which is keyed to records). Promote it as AttendanceGrid when a second module needs it; one consumer is not yet a pattern.
AI assistant composer (suggestion chips + prompt field)Decided Aug 2026 — stays scaffold-local. Its shape is still moving and it appears once. Revisit when a second surface needs a prompt field.
Three pill tracks with three sets of valuesResolved Sep 2026. All three (TabsStrip variant="segmented", SegmentedControl, PageTitleBar's own) now share one bed (--agni-neutral-100), one active state (bg-action-brand, literal #fff label) and one count/badge treatment. PageTitleBar's view-mode switcher keeps its own 32px square button size — a deliberate sizing difference for icon-only targets, not a leftover divergence.
Panel's state props on the sheet and drawer variantsDecided Aug 2026: all three variants. The dispatcher resolves the body once through resolvePanelBody() and hands it down, so PanelBase, Sheet and Drawer cannot drift. Two rules came out of it: the header stays in every state, because an overlay must always be closable; and error / loading withhold the footer, since a sticky Save above an ErrorState offers an action that cannot be performed. empty keeps its footer — the action beside an empty body is usually the way out of it. Previously the four props were documented inline-only and were silently dropped by the overlays.
Default appearance — light, or the operating system's?Decided Aug 2026: the OS. An unattributed document follows prefers-color-scheme; data-theme="light" | "dark" on any element overrides it for that subtree. Specimen cards pin light on <html> so their light halves stay light on a dark-mode machine.
Status vocabularies — two, on purpose

A record's approval state and a task's progress are different questions, so they keep different words. Decided Aug 2026: keep both, and pick by what the surface is about. Never mix strings from the two columns in one control.

VocabularyStringsUse it when
Record statusPending · In Review · Approved · Rejected · On HoldThe question is has this been decided? — list views, record detail, approval queues, RecordCard preset="approval", ApprovalStepper.
Work statusYet to start · In progress · Overdue · Completed · RejectedThe question is how far along is it? — the kanban board, assigned tasks, RecordCard preset="task", Gantt bars.
The board's first column is the seamThe kanban board's Approvals lane holds records still in the record vocabulary — they have not become work yet. Everything to its right is work status. That single lane is where the two vocabularies meet, and it is the only place both appear on one screen.
Both resolve through one tone mapTag status="…" accepts either vocabulary and resolves the tone itself. No module maps a status string to a colour — that is why the two sets can coexist without drifting apart visually.
Role gating — who owns the check

One rule: a component takes role when it owns actions the viewer may not be allowed to perform. Everything else is gated at the call site with RoleGate. Omitting roles on an entry means every role; omitting role on the component means show everything.

Withheld means absent, not disabledA bulk action, an action column or a nav page the role cannot use is not rendered. A disabled control still tells the viewer the capability exists and invites a support ticket. The exception is Tabs, where a disabled tab with "Not available for your role" keeps the strip's shape.
An absent role means ungatedroleAllows("") returns true. A component whose role was never threaded through shows everything, so adding roles metadata to an array can never quietly remove actions from a page that does not gate — the failure would be silent, and silent is the one thing a gate must not be. This gates visibility; the server authorises.
One predicate, seven componentsDropdownMenu · BulkActionToolbar · PageControls · DataTable · StageList · NavRail · RecordDetailModal all call roleAllows(role, allow, deny), which RoleGate itself uses — so the check exists once. Components that render a single unit (ActionTile, OptionRow, AttachmentRow, EventRow) stay call-site gated.
role is overloaded — mind which oneOn the seven above it means access. On PersonCard and ApprovalStep it means job title. Those two predate the access contract and are not renamed, because a person's role genuinely is their role.
Pairs — which one to reach for

FilterPanel

Facets. A funnel popover of multi-select option pills. Quick narrowing, no syntax to learn. Mounts inside PageControls.

FilterBuilder

Rules. AND-chained field · operator · value rows for precise or saved queries. Also mounts inside PageControls.

EmptyState

Nothing here, and that's fine. Says what creates the first item. Never a bare illustration.

ErrorState

Something failed. Says what, offers the way back — message, technical detail, retry.

DataTable

Read, sort, select. The default records view.

EditableTable

Inline add / edit / delete under fixed columns — line items inside a form.

RecordCard

Any record as a card: preset kanban · approval · task. One contract for all three.

PersonCard

A crew member. Deliberately separate — a person is not a record.

PageTitleBar

Page identity: title, scope tabs with counts. Owns the view switcher when both are on screen.

PageControls

Acting on the records: search, filters, sort, columns, bulk selection, pagination.

Record contract

One record shape flows through the whole stack — DataTable rows, RecordCard presets and RecordDetailModal. normalizeRecord() accepts the legacy field names (owner → requestedBy, group → requestedFor, lane → status, type → requestType, raisedOn → date), so older data doesn't need reshaping at the call site.
